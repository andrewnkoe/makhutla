About Lesotho

A small kingdom with big views and a rich culture.

Welcome to the Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is a landlocked country in southern Africa. It is known for its high mountains, traditional culture and outdoor activities.

Visitors can enjoy nature, learn about Basotho culture and meet local people. Visitors should respect local customs and protect the environment.

Culture and People

Basotho culture includes traditional music, crafts, blankets, food and a strong sense of community.

Nature and Landscape

Lesotho is famous for its high mountains, rivers, waterfalls and beautiful countryside.

Responsible Travel

Follow local advice, ask permission before taking pictures of people and keep the environment clean.

Travel Tip

Mountain weather can change quickly. Pack warm clothes and check the weather before travelling.
